> **How to use this template** (delete this block when you are done)
> 1. Adjust the list of systems to what you really use. Start with your invoices and your password manager.
> 2. Fill in the checklist **for each person who leaves** and keep it. A completed, dated checklist is the evidence a reviewer wants to see.
> 3. This also applies to freelancers, interns and people who change roles and no longer need certain access.

## What a reviewer looks for

- **A completed checklist**, not just the empty template. Reviewers often ask about the most recent leaver.
- **A deadline**: how quickly is access gone after the last working day?
- **A check afterwards** (C4): was access really closed everywhere?
- **Shared secrets**: who changes the passwords the leaver could access?
